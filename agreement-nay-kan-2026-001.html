Project changes

Any significant change of scope (change of concept, markedly larger surface, added services) is covered by an amendment specifying the nature of the change and its impact on lead times and fees.

Budget overrun

If the actual CAPEX exceeds the validated estimate by more than 20%, a recalibration meeting is held to adjust the project or its scope.

Suspension

The support may be suspended without penalty in case of a blockage beyond the parties' control, inability to access the site, or the client's decision to suspend the project. Only the phases completed and validated remain due. Amounts already paid are not refundable and remain credited to the Client for the continuation of the journey.

Termination

The Client commits to the journey from signature. Amounts paid are kept by the Consultant and are not refundable. If the Client stops before the end, the network price (−25%) no longer applies: the phases already started are charged at the catalogue price, and if the amounts paid are lower, the difference is due within 7 days. Phases not started are not charged, and the instalments not yet due fall away. Deliverables of paid phases remain the client's property. The Consultant may end this agreement at the end of a validated phase by 14 days' written notice, and then refunds the instalments paid for the phases not carried out.

Confidentiality

Each party keeps the information received from the other strictly confidential, during the agreement and for two years after it ends. The Consultant does not cite the Client or the project publicly without its written consent.

Ownership

The deliverables of the phases that have been paid in full become the property of the Client. The Consultant keeps his pre-existing methods, templates and know-how. The creations of external designers and agencies are governed by their own contracts with the Client.

Responsibility

The Consultant gives advice and coordination with the care of a professional. Budgets, forecasts and profitability plans are estimates based on the information available and are not a guarantee of results, sales or profitability. The Consultant's liability is limited to the fees received for the phase concerned. He acts as an independent contractor, not as an employee, agent or partner of the Client.

Disputes

The parties first try to settle any dispute amicably, by direct discussion, within 30 days of a written notice.

Entire agreement

This document is the entire agreement between the parties for this support. It may be accepted electronically: the acceptance on the next page, with the name, title and date entered, has the value of a signature. The Consultant keeps the accepted version, the signatory's details and the acceptance message received. Any later change is made in writing.
