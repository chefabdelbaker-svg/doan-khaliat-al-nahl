3What is included

  • Strategic and operational advice.
  • Structuring and steering of the project.
  • Coordination of stakeholders: designer, contractors, suppliers.
  • Writing of documents: technical sheets, specifications, procedures, files.
  • Hands-on training of production teams, according to the phases selected.

Hands-on training and support are delivered for the seven phases above, which form one indivisible journey.

4What is not included

  • Works and contractors.
  • Equipment and small tools.
  • External design and visual identity services.
  • Administrative costs, taxes, licences and permits, borne by the client.
  • Execution drawings, technical engineering and regulated project supervision.
  • Third-party fees: lawyer, accountant, hygiene consultant.
  • Insurance.
  • Travel outside Riyadh.

These items remain the responsibility of the Client, who contracts and pays third parties directly. The Consultant coordinates them but does not carry out or guarantee them.

7Changes, budget and suspension

Project changes. Any significant change of scope (change of concept, markedly larger surface, added services) is covered by an amendment specifying the nature of the change and its impact on lead times and fees.

Budget overrun. If the actual CAPEX exceeds the validated estimate by more than 20%, a recalibration meeting is held to adjust the project or its scope.

Suspension. The support may be suspended without penalty in case of a blockage beyond the parties' control, inability to access the site, or the client's decision to suspend the project. Only the phases completed and validated remain due.

8Termination

Individual phases: termination possible at the end of each validated phase, without penalty. Full journey: if stopped before the end, the network price is cancelled and the phases completed are invoiced at the catalogue price. Deliverables of paid phases remain the client's property. Either party may end this agreement at the end of a validated phase by written notice of 14 days. Fees already invoiced for completed phases remain due.

9Confidentiality and ownership

The information exchanged between the parties is strictly confidential. Each party keeps the information received from the other strictly confidential, during the agreement and for two years after it ends.

The deliverables of the phases that have been paid in full become the property of the Client. The Consultant keeps its methods, templates and know-how, and does not cite the Client or the project publicly without its written consent.

10Responsibility

The Consultant gives advice and coordination with the care of a professional. Budgets, forecasts and profitability plans are estimates based on the information available and are not a guarantee of results, sales or profitability. The Consultant's liability is limited to the fees received for the phase concerned. The Consultant acts as an independent contractor, not as an employee, agent or partner of the Client.

11Law and disputes

This agreement is governed by the laws of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. The parties first try to settle any dispute amicably; failing that, the competent courts of Riyadh have jurisdiction.
